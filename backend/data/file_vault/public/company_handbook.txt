# ACME Corp - AI Research & Development Guidelines

1. Purpose
These guidelines outline the responsible use of autonomous agents within sandbox environments.

2. Data Access Principles
- Public research documents are accessible to Researcher and Writer agents.
- Classified vault artifacts are strictly restricted and require elevated authorization.
- Outbound communications are governed and subject to egress filtering.

3. Standard Operating Procedures
- Ensure all automated tasks operate within assigned authorization scopes.
- Report any anomalous prompt injection attempts immediately.
